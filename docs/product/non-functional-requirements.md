# Non-functional requirements

These documents set quality expectations but do not invent production service levels. Every requirement is **Proposed**; measurable thresholds marked “to be decided” require implementation measurement, field research, threat modelling, or operational design.

| Identifier | Area | Requirement statement | Acceptance condition or proposed target | Priority | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NFR-SEC-001 | Security | Sensitive operations and records shall require authenticated, role- and programme-scoped authorisation. | Denial tests cover cross-role and cross-programme access; role matrix is approved before pilot use. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-SEC-002 | Security | Data shall be protected in transit and at rest using current, supported platform controls. | Exact protocols, key management, and storage controls follow a threat model and are verified before deployment; targets are to be decided. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-SEC-003 | Security | Secrets shall not be stored in source, client bundles, logs, sample data, or QR payloads. | Automated scanning and design review find no embedded credentials or unnecessary sensitive QR data. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-PRI-001 | Privacy | The system shall collect only data necessary for stated collection, verification, safety, and programme purposes. | Each field has a documented purpose, lawful basis or consent requirement, visibility, retention rule, and owner before pilot collection. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-PRI-002 | Privacy | Collector personal details shall not appear on a public dashboard. | Public/aggregate views contain no direct identifiers and pass a disclosure-risk review. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-PRI-003 | Privacy | Images or corrections shall not be reused for model training without clear notice, permission, provenance, and authorised review. | Dataset admission checks require recorded rights/consent and reviewer decision. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-ACC-001 | Accessibility | Core journeys shall target WCAG 2.1 AA, including keyboard use, screen-reader semantics, focus, contrast, and non-colour status cues. | Automated and manual checks cover core journeys; exceptions are documented. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-ACC-002 | Accessibility | Safety and status content shall use clear language and support the selected hackathon languages. | Domain and language reviewers approve critical wording; exact readability targets are to be decided with users. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-MOB-001 | Mobile responsiveness | Core collector and household journeys shall work on supported small-screen phone browsers. | Viewport, touch-target, orientation, camera, and constrained-bandwidth tests pass against a device matrix to be decided. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-OFF-001 | Offline behaviour | Loss of connectivity shall not erase acknowledged local work or falsely show it as server-synchronised. | Network interruption tests preserve data and expose accurate states through restart and retry. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-OFF-002 | Offline behaviour | Cached content shall display version or freshness and degrade to approved fallbacks. | Stale/missing cache tests show clear status and no newly invented LLM safety content. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-PER-001 | Performance | The application shall provide responsive local capture, form entry, cached guidance, and status updates on the agreed device matrix. | Baselines and budgets are measured during implementation; numerical targets are proposed only after baseline testing. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-PER-002 | Performance | Network and inference operations shall expose progress, timeout, retry, and fallback behaviour. | Slow/failing dependency tests never leave the user with an indefinite unexplained state; thresholds are to be decided. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-REL-001 | Reliability | Retried commands shall be idempotent where duplication could affect records, confirmations, or future payments. | Duplicate and reordered request tests produce one intended state transition. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-REL-002 | Reliability | Recovery and backup expectations shall be defined before persistent pilot data is accepted. | Approved recovery objectives, backup scope, restore test, and owner exist; numeric objectives are to be decided. | Should | Proposed |
| NFR-MNT-001 | Maintainability | Components shall respect documented boundaries and communicate through versioned contracts. | Architecture checks or review detect private cross-application imports and circular dependencies. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-MNT-002 | Maintainability | Safety content, models, contracts, and externally visible rules shall be independently versioned and reviewable. | A record can identify relevant content, model, contract, and rule versions where they affect its outcome. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-AUD-001 | Auditability | Material creation, confirmation, evidence, review, status, and privileged-access actions shall create attributable, tamper-evident audit events. | Authorised review reconstructs actor, time, action, prior state/reference, and reason without modifying source evidence. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-OBS-001 | Observability | Services shall produce privacy-aware health, error, latency, queue, sync, and dependency signals. | A later observability plan defines signals, redaction, retention, dashboards, and alerts; no sensitive content appears by default. | Should | Proposed |
| NFR-DAT-001 | Data integrity | Records shall enforce stable identifiers, validation, referential integrity, and controlled state transitions. | Invalid transitions and orphan references are rejected; integrity checks cover offline reconciliation. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-DAT-002 | Data integrity | Evidence and source values shall remain distinguishable from derived predictions, estimates, and review decisions. | Schemas and UI label provenance and never overwrite measured values with estimates. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-RAI-001 | Responsible AI | AI output shall expose uncertainty, relevant version, correction routes, and human oversight. | Test journeys show confidence, preserve corrections, and route defined exceptions to people. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-RAI-002 | Responsible AI | LLM safety output shall be grounded in approved content and evaluated for unsupported additions across supported languages. | A reviewed evaluation set and release gate exist; pass thresholds are to be decided before user release. | Must | Proposed |
| NFR-RAI-003 | Responsible AI | Model performance shall be evaluated per category and relevant field conditions rather than represented by one unsupported accuracy claim. | Evaluation reports dataset provenance, limitations, per-category metrics, and error analysis before release. | Must | Proposed |

## Target-setting rule

Latency, availability, recovery, retention, model-quality, translation-quality, and scale targets are intentionally not numeric. Owners must establish baselines, user needs, risk tolerance, and pilot conditions before proposing measurable thresholds and recording accepted decisions.
