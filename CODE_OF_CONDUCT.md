# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

ScrapTrace contributors are expected to maintain a respectful, inclusive, and safe working environment. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and demeaning conduct are not acceptable.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Respect affected communities, collectors, households, recyclers, and data subjects.
- Handle safety, personal data, and AI limitations responsibly.
- Give constructive feedback and acknowledge mistakes.
- Do not publish private, sensitive, or unapproved project information.

## Reporting and enforcement

The reporting contact, response process, and enforcement owners are **to be decided** before external contribution begins. Until then, concerns should be raised privately with the project lead. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect contributors and users.

This initial policy will be reviewed before public participation.
